{# ── Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) ── #}
Support & Guidance

Frequently Asked Questions

{# Q1 #}

Do I need to fast before my blood test?

Tests like Fasting Blood Sugar, Lipid Profiles, and comprehensive wellness panels require 8 to 12 hours of overnight fasting. You may drink plain water. Each test in our catalog clearly indicates fasting requirements.

{# Q2 #}

How soon will my digital diagnostic report be available?

Most routine hematology and biochemistry panels (CBC, LFT, RFT) are released within 4 to 12 hours. As soon as our Senior Pathologist signs off on the results, you will receive an in-app notification and your downloadable PDF will unlock.

{# Q3 #}

How are abnormal biomarker results reviewed?

Our system automatically flags values that fall outside standard reference intervals as LOW or HIGH. The pathologist independently reviews flagged samples and may order a re-run from the archived primary tube if clinical confirmation is warranted.

{# Q4 #}

How do I book a home sample collection?

Simply register or log in, click "Book a Test", select "Home Visit" as your collection method, and enter your street address and preferred time slot. A phlebotomist will be dispatched accordingly.
